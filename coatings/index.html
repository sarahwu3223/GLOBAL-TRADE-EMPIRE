MK/MZ TECHNOLOGY

Coating systems for severe corrosion environments

Liaoning Fuxin Anti-Corrosion Materials Technology Co., Ltd. describes its business as integrating anti-corrosion coating research and manufacturing, coating engineering, equipment design and manufacturing, technology transfer, and technical consulting. Its MK/MZ technology is designed for project-specific service environments and application processes.

Solvent-free systems

Two principal coating families: fusion-bonded epoxy powder and two-component liquid epoxy for room-temperature curing.

Thick-film protection

The technical brochure describes coating thickness options selected according to project requirements and coating process.

Process integration

Heating and curing processes, ambient-temperature application, and coating equipment are matched to the workpiece and project.

PRODUCT PORTFOLIO

MK/MZ coating product families

The following categories are based on the company's technical introduction. Exact grades, service limits, test reports and availability should be confirmed for each inquiry.

01 · POWDER COATING

Fusion-Bonded Epoxy Powder Coatings

High-performance powder coating technology with a stated curing process around 230°C. The brochure lists anti-corrosion, abrasion-resistant, rapid-curing, anti-cavitation/erosion, rebar and strand protection, acid-resistant and hygienic coating types.

02 · LIQUID COATING

Solvent-Free Liquid Epoxy Coatings

Two-component liquid epoxy coating designed for room-temperature curing. Listed types include corrosion-resistant repair compounds, thick-film field-joint coatings, acid-resistant coatings, hygienic coatings, gas-pipeline drag-reduction/wear coatings and concrete protection coatings.

03 · ENGINEERED SYSTEM

Project-Specific Coating Systems

Coating selection and process planning according to the corrosive medium, operating temperature, substrate, service environment and project durability requirements.

04 · APPLICATION SUPPORT

Coating Process & Equipment

The company brochure describes coating line design for pipelines, structural steel, reinforcing bars, special-shaped components, sheet piles and steel strands.

APPLICATION AREAS

Designed for demanding infrastructure and industry

Applications identified in the company technical introduction include:

Marine & offshore

Marine structures, offshore platforms, subsea pipelines, ports, seawater equipment and ship-related equipment.

Pipelines & utilities

Buried and exposed pipelines for water, drainage, heating, gas, oil and other industrial media.

Bridges & infrastructure

Bridge piles, tunnels, pipe corridors, water infrastructure and large civil engineering structures.

Energy

Onshore and offshore wind power, photovoltaic projects and related steel components.

Industrial equipment

Pumps, valves, fans, expansion joints, tanks, boilers and water-treatment equipment.

Reinforced concrete

Epoxy-coated reinforcing bars and strands for concrete structures in corrosive environments.
