1. Company and technology overview

The brochure describes Liaoning Fuxin Anti-Corrosion Materials Technology Co., Ltd. as an enterprise integrating anti-corrosion coating R&D and production, coating application engineering, equipment design and manufacture, commercialization of technical results and technical consulting. It describes cooperation with the Corrosion and Protection Center of Northeastern University.

It identifies two principal product families: high-performance fusion-bonded epoxy powder coatings and two-component, solvent-free liquid epoxy polymer-composite coatings designed for ambient-temperature curing. The brochure says formulations are selected according to service environment and project requirements.

2. Product families

Fusion-bonded epoxy powder coatings

Brochure-stated curing temperature: approximately 230°C. Listed types include high-corrosion-resistance, abrasion-resistant, rapid-curing, anti-cavitation/erosion, reinforcing-bar and steel-strand protection, strong-acid-resistant and hygienic coatings.

Solvent-free liquid epoxy coatings

Two-component, ambient-temperature-curing systems. Listed types include corrosion-resistant repair compounds, thick-film field-joint coatings, strong-acid-resistant coatings, hygienic coatings, natural-gas pipeline drag-reduction/wear coatings and high-pressure airless-spray concrete protection coatings.

Application process options stated in the brochure

  • Heated rapid-curing application, described for factory-scale pipeline and structural-component production.
  • Heated slow-curing application, described for more demanding temperature and chemical environments.
  • Conventional heat-curing application.
  • Ambient-temperature curing, including high-pressure airless spraying or trowel application for field work.

4. Production and coating equipment

The brochure says the company has developed and designed coating lines for different engineering requirements, including:

  1. Internal and external pipeline coating lines for fusion-bonded epoxy powder.
  2. Large-diameter pipeline powder-coating lines.
  3. Structural steel and reinforcing-bar powder-coating lines.
  4. Special-shaped component coating lines.
  5. Steel sheet-pile coating lines.
  6. Automatic liquid-coating pipeline lines.
  7. Epoxy powder coating lines for steel strands.

Equipment and structures exposed to severe corrosion environments.

9. Information needed for an export quotation

  • Substrate and component geometry.
  • Corrosive medium and concentration; immersion, splash, buried or atmospheric exposure.
  • Operating and application temperatures.
  • Required coating family: fusion-bonded powder or solvent-free liquid epoxy.
  • Target dry-film thickness and application method.
  • Applicable project standard, inspection requirements and required certificates.
  • Quantity, destination country and delivery schedule.
